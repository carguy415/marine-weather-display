Step 1 · USB installation

Flash your ESP32

Connect a classic ESP32-WROOM-32 DevKit to your computer using a data USB cable. Open this page in desktop Chrome or Edge and click Install. The firmware is the same for all supported NOAA forecast zones.

Installing may erase existing code and device settings. Back up any sketch you want to keep.

Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer with Web Serial support. The installer requires HTTPS. Visit this site through its secure GitHub Pages link.

The browser sends the firmware to your ESP32 over USB. This page does not ask for, see, or store your home Wi-Fi password.

Steps 2–4 · Local setup

Personalize your marine forecast

Find your zone first: Open NOAA's Coastal Marine Forecast Zone Map, choose your coastal area, and note the six-character zone code (such as ANZ331) before joining the ESP32 temporary setup Wi-Fi.

2

Find the setup Wi-Fi

After installation, the screen shows a network named MARINEWX-... and a randomly generated password for that particular ESP32. The password stays the same every time you return to setup.

3

Use your phone

Join that network, then open http://192.168.4.1. Enter your home Wi-Fi name, password, NOAA marine zone, and preferred backlight timeout. Tap the arrow in the zone field to pick from saved zones; press and hold a saved zone to delete it.

4

Save and enjoy

The ESP32 restarts and downloads the forecast. Press BOOT for the next period; the screen goes dark after your chosen timeout.

To change Wi-Fi, marine zone, or backlight timeout later, hold BOOT for 4 seconds while the device is running. The setup network reappears using the same saved password, so your phone can remember it. The password is always displayed on the TFT. Example coastal forecast zones: ANZ331, ANZ335, ANZ232. The first version also recognizes AMZ, GMZ, and PZZ coastal forecast codes when NOAA publishes compatible text files.

Don't hold BOOT while plugging in or resetting the ESP32, since GPIO0 also controls programming mode. Do not rely on this hobby display as your only source of safety-critical marine forecasts or warnings.

Hardware and wiring

ESP32 DevKit + ST7789 2.0-inch, 240 × 320 SPI display with pins GND, VCC, SCL, SDA, RST, DC, CS, BL. Connections: GND→GND, VCC→3V3, SCL→GPIO18, SDA→GPIO23, RST→GPIO22, DC→GPIO19, CS→GPIO5, BL→GPIO27.

BL to GPIO27 is based on a measured 2.4mA backlight control connection on the original display. Check the electrical design of alternative modules before connecting their BL pins directly to an ESP32 GPIO.

Find your NOAA coastal forecast zone using the interactive Coastal Marine Forecast Zone Map. More details are in the project README.
